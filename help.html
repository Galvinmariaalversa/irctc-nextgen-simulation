Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my booking failing?

Booking failures can occur due to: (1) Network connectivity issues, (2) Session timeout, (3) Invalid station selection, (4) Insufficient balance in payment account. Try refreshing and booking again. If issue persists, contact support.

How to change the date of journey?

To modify your journey date: Log in → Go to My Bookings → Select the ticket → Click "Modify Booking" → Choose new date (subject to availability and modification charges if applicable) → Confirm changes.

What is Premium Tatkal?

Premium Tatkal (PT) allows booking of premium coaches (1A, 2A, 3A) up to 180 days in advance. Charges are higher than regular Tatkal. Suitable for business travelers and those planning long journeys in advance.

Can I book multiple tickets in one transaction?

Yes, you can book up to 6 passengers in a single transaction. Enter passenger details, select seats if required, and proceed to payment.

Payment deducted but ticket not booked – what to do?

If your payment is deducted but booking fails: (1) Check your registered email for confirmation. (2) If no confirmation within 5 minutes, don't retry – contact support. (3) Refund will be processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method.

What is the refund timeline for cancelled tickets?

After cancellation: Debit/Credit cards → 5-7 business days | Net Banking → 2-3 business days | UPI → 1-2 business days | Wallet → Instant. Cancellation charges apply as per IRCTC policy.

Why is my UPI/NetBanking refund delayed?

Refunds can be delayed due to: (1) Bank server issues, (2) Incorrect account details, (3) Holiday periods. If refund doesn't arrive within stipulated time, contact your bank and provide IRCTC transaction ID.

Can I get a refund after my train has departed?

No. Once the train departs, no refund is possible. However, you can claim compensation if the train was cancelled or significantly delayed. File a claim through "Help & Support" section in your account.

How to reset my forgotten password?

On login page, click "Forgot Password" → Enter your registered email/mobile → You'll receive an OTP on your email or SMS → Enter OTP → Set a new password → Login with new credentials.

My account is locked. How to unlock it?

Account gets locked after 5 incorrect login attempts for security. Wait 24 hours, or use "Forgot Password" to reset and regain access immediately. Contact support if issue persists.

Email/Phone verification failed. What should I do?

Check if you entered the correct OTP (valid for 10 minutes). Ensure your email and mobile number are correct in account settings. If OTP not received, request a new OTP or contact support.

Can I change my registered email or mobile number?

Yes. Go to Account Settings → Edit Profile → Change email/mobile → Verify with OTP → Save changes. Your account will remain active during this process.

How to check live train status?

Go to "Quick Links" → "Live Train Status" → Enter train number/name → Select date → View real-time location, current status, and expected arrival/departure times at each station.

What are train running days?

Running days show which days a train operates (Monday, Tuesday, etc.). Search for trains based on your preferred travel date to see which trains run on that day. Some trains don't run on certain days.

How to check seat availability?

Enter from/to stations, date, and class → Click "Search Trains" → Each train shows available seats. Green = Available | Yellow = Limited | Red = Not Available. Availability updates in real-time.

What does my PNR status mean?

CNF = Confirmed | RAC = Reservation Against Cancellation (waitlist) | WL = Waiting List. RAC and WL passengers may get confirmed as other passengers cancel.

What are IRCTC cancellation charges?

Charges depend on days before travel: 0-1 days = 50% + GST | 2-3 days = 25% + GST | 4-7 days = 10% + GST | 8+ days = 5% + GST. Tatkal charges don't apply to Tatkal bookings.

When is Tatkal booking available?

Tatkal bookings open at 10:00 AM (for trains departing next day). Tatkal charges apply + additional Tatkal fee. Premium Tatkal opens 10 days in advance at 10:00 AM.

Can RAC tickets be cancelled?

Yes. RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) tickets can be cancelled with charges: Before 48 hours = 50% refund | Within 48 hours = No refund.

What's the difference between Tatkal and Premium Tatkal?

Tatkal: 2A/3A/SL coaches, booking 1 day before | Premium Tatkal: 1A/2A/3A, booking up to 180 days in advance. PT charges are higher but offer more options and classes.
